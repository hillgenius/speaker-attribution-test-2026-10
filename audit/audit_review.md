# Reference audit: 200 agreeing turns

Drawn by `audit_sample.py` with the beacon seed (`audit_sample.json`, 22,790 agreeing turns across 100 hearings, weighted by word count). Each turn was read against the print (header line, the line before it, the turn's first and last words, and for every turn over 1,000 words a scan of the print lines inside it for a second speaker header). Nothing from the scored transcript was used. Identity was checked too: every Bioguide id the 100 references resolved was compared with the print's own member list (`audit_bioguides.py`, output `results/bioguide_check.txt`). The 46 pairs it could not confirm automatically were read by hand: 42 are right (state names that wrap to the next print line, abbreviated states, members testifying at a Member Day) and 4 are wrong. Three share one cause: `members.cl_last` is null for Mark Green (TN) and Sylvester Turner (TX), so the roster matched the only same-surname member it could (Al Green, Michael Turner): 60649 Green, 60649 Turner and 60692 Turner. One of their turns falls in this sample ([54]). The fourth is a print error: 59601 prints "Mr. Murray." ten times for the witness Nicholas McMurray, and the roster resolved that to Senator Patty Murray; its only cluster was discarded as ambiguous, and no turn of it is in this sample. The only other null-`cl_last` surname collision among 119th-Congress members (Raul/Adelita Grijalva) resolves correctly in both hearings where it occurs (December 2025, after Adelita Grijalva was seated).
